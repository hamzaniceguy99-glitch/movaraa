How we work

Off the snow, and clear about the limits

This is video coaching. Nobody is on the hill with you, and remote coaching can't judge the snow, the light or the traffic on a run.

So take on-snow lessons from a certified instructor, wear a helmet, and stay on open marked terrain. We don't teach backcountry travel, avalanche terrain or rescue — that needs in-person avalanche education with a recognized provider, and nothing online substitutes for it.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Is this instead of a ski lesson?

No. On-snow lessons with a certified instructor do things video can't. This is coaching between ski days — footage analysis, drills, conditioning and a plan — which lessons rarely include.

Will you teach me backcountry skiing?

No. Avalanche terrain requires in-person certified avalanche education, a partner, and rescue gear you've practiced with. We'll point you to providers instead.

Ski or snowboard?

Both. The coaching principles overlap more than people think, and sessions are split by discipline where technique differs.

How do I get footage?

A friend filming from behind, a follow-cam, or a phone on a pole. Film wide, in decent light, and include the whole turn rather than close-ups.

I only ski a week a year.

Then planning matters more, not less. Pre-season conditioning plus a clear focus for each day is how a single week produces real change.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no minimum term. Access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for.
