The programs, in detail

Four programs of 6 to 12 weeks, built around the days you actually get on snow.

Program 01 · Edges, not skids

Carving clean

Skidded turns come from steering with the feet and leaning back. We work from your footage on stance, on tipping early, and on where pressure builds through the turn — plus the drills that make it stick on a groomed blue before you take it anywhere steeper.

  • 10 weeks
  • Confident on blues
  • 3 hours a week
  • Your own skis or board

Start this program

Tipping before the fall line.

Weeks 7–8 · Pressure

Building and releasing it through the turn.

Holding the carve as the pitch increases.

Weeks 1–3 · Short turns

Quick edge changes on moderate pitch.

Weeks 4–7 · Absorption

Legs working, upper body calm.

Weeks 8–10 · Line choice

Reading a bump field before you drop in.

Weeks 11–12 · Steeps

Committing down the hill, safely and on open terrain.

Program 02 · Terrain that punishes

Bumps & steeps

Bumps and steeps expose everything: late edging, wide stance, static upper body. We rebuild the short turn, work on absorption and line choice through moguls, and get you looking three bumps ahead instead of at the one you're on.

  • 12 weeks
  • Solid on blacks
  • 4 hours a week
  • Poles, and a helmet

Start this program

Program 03 · Ski what's actually there

Conditions & mountain sense

The same technique doesn't work on boilerplate ice and afternoon slush. We cover how each snow type changes what you do, how to plan a day around aspect and sun, reading grooming reports, and skiing responsibly around other people. Marked, open terrain only.

  • 8 weeks
  • Any level
  • 2 hours a week
  • A season pass helps

Start this program

Weeks 1–2 · Snow types

Weeks 3–4 · Planning a day

Aspect, sun and timing your runs.

Flat light, fog and skiing by feel.

Weeks 7–8 · Responsibility

Right of way, merging and skiing around others.

Weeks 1–3 · Base

Quads, glutes and hips, built gradually.

Weeks 4–5 · Eccentric work

The strength that stops legs blowing up by lunch.

Weeks 6–7 · Balance

Single-leg control, which is all skiing is.

Week 8 · Taper

Arriving fresh rather than sore.

Program 04 · Don't waste week one

Pre-season prep

Arriving unfit costs you the first half of a trip and raises injury risk exactly when you're least prepared. We build an eight-week plan of eccentric leg work, hip strength and balance you can do in a small space with no equipment worth mentioning.

  • 8 weeks
  • Any level
  • 3 hours a week
  • Floor space
